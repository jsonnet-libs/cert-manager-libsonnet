    fn spec.withRenewBefore

    withRenewBefore(renewBefore)

    "How long before the currently issued certificate's expiry cert-manager should\nrenew the certificate.     fn spec.withRenewBeforePercentage

    withRenewBeforePercentage(renewBeforePercentage)

    "renewBeforePercentage is like renewBefore, except it is a relative percentage\nrather than an absolute duration.
